About the platform

One campus.
Every org, in one place.

PLMun Verse is where Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Muntinlupa's student organizations post events, open recruitment, and get found — so joining one is never a matter of who you happen to know.

Why we built this

At PLMun, recruitment season looks the same every year: tarpaulins along the walkway, group chats you find out about too late, and sign-up sheets that never make it past the org's own members. Good orgs lose good applicants simply because the posting never reached them.

PLMun Verse puts every organization's events, openings, and announcements on one shared board. Students browse by interest instead of by luck, and orgs reach the whole campus instead of just their own network.

The name carries the idea: PLMun stays one university, but the "verse" is every org's voice, posted on equal footing.

What you can do here

Built for both sides of recruitment

Students look for a place to belong. Orgs look for people who'll show up. PLMun Verse gives each side the tools for their half of that.

For students

Find your org, not just an org

  • Browse every recognized organization's page in one directory
  • Follow orgs to get their events and recruitment posts as they go up
  • See requirements, deadlines, and schedules without hunting through group chats
  • Apply or register for an event directly from the post
For orgs

Reach the campus, not just your circle

  • Publish recruitment drives and events to students actively looking
  • Manage your org's profile, officers, and standing announcements
  • Track who's interested and who's applied in one place
  • Post updates once — no re-posting across five different group chats
